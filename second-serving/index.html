Private by design. Honest by default.

Photos are processed in memory and are not uploaded or saved. The vision libraries and model are downloaded from jsDelivr and Google when you scan; those services receive ordinary network metadata, not your photos. No analytics, accounts or backend.

We use TensorFlow.js and the COCO-SSD model, not a food-specialist classifier. It supports five produce categories here: apple, banana, orange, broccoli and carrot. Confidence is a model score, not proof. Samples use clearly labelled preset detections.

This is a meal-planning tool, not a food-safety test. It cannot determine ripeness, spoilage, allergens or safe storage. “Use first” is your planning priority, not an expiry prediction. Follow packaging and local food-safety guidance; never use food you suspect is unsafe.

Ingredient weights start as editable suggestions. The impact counter totals only weights you confirm as used; it does not measure prevented waste or emissions. Shelf and history remain in this browser’s local storage until you remove them or clear site data. Other apps on the same GitHub Pages origin share that storage origin. If storage is unavailable, the app works for the current session only.
