A GUIDE TO THE OTHER SIDE

Float. Focus. Repeat.

  1. Find your lift.

    Space, ↑, click or tap the playfield to float upward. Stop tapping to fall. Avoid the luminous columns and the upper and lower boundaries.

  2. Thread the portals.

    Each gate earns 1 distance point. Pass within 22 pixels of its center for a perfect pass. Consecutive perfect passes build your streak and restore 15% focus energy each.

  3. Slow your universe.

    Hold Shift or the on-screen Focus button to run at 45% speed. A full meter gives 2.5 seconds of focus; it recharges in 10 seconds when released. After it empties, release before activating again.

  4. Make it your own.

    Chill, Flow and Warp have separate best scores. Three color worlds change every 8 portals. Settings include quiet visuals, sound and a starting color world.

P / ESC Pause R Retry after a run M Sound
