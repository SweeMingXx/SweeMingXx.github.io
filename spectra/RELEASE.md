# SPECTRA — InnovArt demonstration release

Open the studio: **https://sweemingxx.github.io/spectra/**

## The value proposition

One creative idea, experienced in more than one way. SPECTRA makes a palette simultaneously visible, audible and describable, with no musical training, account or paid service required.

The strongest fit is **transforming artistic expressions across media and sensory modalities**, alongside interactive art, personalized creative experiences, creative learning and adaptive-art exploration.

## What to show judges

1. Start with Amber tides. Play the soundscape, then use keys 1–5 to isolate each color's note.
2. Switch to Your experience. Explain the color score and the shared representation behind sound, text and visual form; pause motion without losing sound.
3. Import an image you have permission to use. Its locally extracted colors immediately change both the visual work and its score.
4. Shape and name a variation. Save it, export its labeled score or 16-second soundscape, and open its share link in another browser.

## Boundaries to explain honestly

- The color-to-note mapping is an artistic design, not a scientific assertion about synesthesia.
- The description explains the generated composition, not the semantic content of imported images.
- Saved work lives in browser storage. JSON export/import is the portable backup route.
- Browser speech support varies; text descriptions always remain available.
- Accessibility co-design and research with disabled artists is a next step, not completed validation.
- No competition outcome is promised. The working prototype and its repeatable demonstration are the submission foundation.
