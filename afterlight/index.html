A QUICK TRANSMISSION

Meet your
future self.

  1. Steer. Drag anywhere in the arena like a joystick. Your light follows the direction of your drag. Desktop: WASD or arrow keys.
  2. Collect. Diamonds are worth 100 points. Collect within 4 seconds to build a multiplier, up to 5×.
  3. Adapt. Every 8 seconds, a coral echo repeats a route you just took. You have 3 shields. Survive 60 seconds to escape.
  4. Pulse. Every 4 shards charge a pulse. Tap PULSE or press Space for 2 seconds of protection.

Daily uses the same UTC-date seed for everyone. Zen has no damage or ranked score. Scores stay on this device; there is no online leaderboard.

Tip: move in wide loops, then change your route when the next echo arrives. Release your thumb to stop.
