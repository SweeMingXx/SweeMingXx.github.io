A NOTE ON EDITORIAL CARE

Nothing silently
left behind.

This independent reading edition brings together all 14 uploaded PDF reports. Running page furniture is removed, line wrapping is repaired, and tables and diagram labels are reformatted for reading. The complete narrative, recommendations, figures and quotations are retained; the original PDF visual layouts are not reproduced here.

Documents are accounts supplied for this report, not independently verified transcripts. Where filenames, figures or descriptions conflict, the source wording is preserved and an editorial note appears in the reader. A second panel upload with identical extracted text remains separately indexed.

The programme also includes sessions for which no standalone report was supplied. These are labelled as programme-only entries, not filled with invented reporting.
