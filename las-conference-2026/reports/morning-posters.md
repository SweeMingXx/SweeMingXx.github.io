## SMU Libraries: Multi-Tiered AI Feedback Intelligence
Presenting on behalf of Singapore Management University (SMU) Libraries, William Koh tackled the chronic operational challenge of parsing thousands of unstructured annual patron inquiries and complaints. Spanning issues from noise disruptions and seat hogging to citation errors and electronic resource licensing failures, qualitative feedback is historically difficult to visualize on conventional tabular dashboards without labor-intensive manual analysis.

SMU developed an internal, staff-facing smart dashboard powered by a sequential three-layer AI architecture. This automated system ingests free-text feedback and maps entries into four operational categories: emerging issues (systemic faults), behavioral insights (usage patterns), unmet patron expectations, and actionable solutions. As feedback flows into the platform, the integrated agents continuously refine their clustering accuracy, giving managers real-time diagnostic visibility.

### SMU Libraries: three-layer AI feedback pipeline
- Raw Feedback Ingestion — Unstructured enquiries & tickets
- Layer 1: Categorization — Automated issue tagging
- Layer 2: Directive AI — Trend extraction & 4-part summary
- Layer 3: Staff Copilot — Secure interactive Q&A

### Architecture & Decision Support Impact
The architecture operates through three tiers: Layer 1 runs automated categorization to assign taxonomy tags across submissions. Layer 2 deploys directive AI to condense volumes of feedback into executive summaries highlighting chronic anomalies. If frontline managers require deeper inquiry, Layer 3 provides a sandboxed conversational Copilot assistant that queries verified ticket logs without exposing private user data.

This workflow eliminates manual data sorting, enabling library administration to address operational pain points rapidly. Facility managers can pinpoint specific floors facing recurring noise violations or identify digital collections experiencing authentication friction, shifting library administration from passive response to proactive service remediation.

## SIT: Biophilic Transformation & Shelfless Operations
Nay Tan outlined the radical spatial overhaul executed at the Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT) Library. In response to declining checkouts of physical shelf stock and heightened demand for study hubs, SIT surveyed student cohorts to redesign their footprint around human well-being, zoning flexibility, and biophilic architectural principles.

The facility was organized into three distinct acoustic and experiential zones: silent zones for deep individual study, enclosed pods for team collaboration, and a "chill corner" incorporating natural greenery, warm organic tones, and soothing ambient audio. Physical stacks were transitioned to remote high-density storage supported by a self-service "collect and return" workflow, demonstrating that physical footprint reduction can expand user satisfaction when paired with intuitive logistics.

## Ngee Ann Poly: Quadruple-Bottom-Line Sustainability
Dinson Seng presented the sustainability blueprint developed by Lian Ying Chow Library at Ngee Ann Polytechnic. Grounded in a four-dimensional framework, the library positions environmental, social, economic, and human sustainability as an integrated institutional operating model.

### Global Distinction: IFLA Green Library Award 2026
Lian Ying Chow Library achieved 3rd place at the IFLA Green Library Award 2026 in Busan, alongside the MOE Energy Commendation Award. Guided by their campus mascot Billy the Bee, the initiative demonstrates how vocational libraries can lead ecological stewardship and community engagement across Singapore.

The library operates as an unmanned facility supported by "LibBee", an in-house generative AI assistant. Social memory is preserved through digital galleries, while the "Airudites" student championship network delivers peer literacy programs, circular book exchanges, and financial wellness workshops.

## Strategic Directives for Academic & Applied Libraries
- Implement Multi-Layer AI Decision Pipelines: Move beyond basic keyword trackers by pairing classification algorithms with conversational staff Copilots to transform unstructured feedback into targeted service improvements.
- Decouple Physical Stacks from Learning Environments: Transition low-circulation print holdings into automated offsite facilities, reclaiming square footage for biophilic rest spaces and acoustic-controlled collaboration zones.
- Align Library Blueprints with Quadruple Sustainability: Embed environmental energy efficiency, social memory preservation, economic automation, and human wellness directly into institutional reporting benchmarks.
- Mobilize Student Ambassador Networks: Empower student advocacy groups (such as the Airudites) to lead peer-to-peer digital literacy and sustainability campaigns, building organic community ownership.
