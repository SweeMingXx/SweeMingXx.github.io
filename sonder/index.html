Privacy, accessibility & honest boundaries

All artwork and audio are generated on this device. Saved work uses this browser’s local storage; it does not sync and can be lost if browser data is cleared. Export a project to back it up. GitHub serves the app and may log normal web requests. There is no analytics or advertising code.

Sound starts only when you choose it. Animation respects reduced-motion settings and can be paused. All creative controls work with a keyboard. Descriptions explain composition but cannot convey every visual detail. Speech depends on your browser’s voices. This is a creative tool, not medical care or a validated therapy.

Original procedural visuals and synthesized audio; no stock artwork or training dataset. A seed is a reproducibility aid, not proof of ownership. Work is not posted to any public gallery.

A TWO-MINUTE CREATIVE EXERCISE

Notice. Shape. Keep.

  1. Notice one small thing.

    Light on a wall. The sound of rain. A conversation that stayed with you. Write a few words in the studio.

  2. Follow your own associations.

    Choose a feeling, then move Energy from one end to the other. What changes? Keep the setting that feels closest.

  3. Try another language.

    Listen to the sound, or read the description. Switch the form. Notice how the same idea changes without disappearing.

  4. Give this moment a home.

    Name your artwork and keep it. There is no score, no streak, and no wrong answer.
