A quick field guide.

You are Webwing, an original rooftop courier with a prototype silk-powered flight suit. Your mission? Keep flying.

  • Space / ↑ / click / tap: flap upward. Gravity handles the rest.
  • P / Escape: pause or resume. R: retry after a crash. M: toggle sound.
  • Pass a pair of towers to score 1 point. Avoid the roof, floor, and tower edges.
  • Collect gold sparks for suits. Every seventh gate carries a cyan shield: one protected collision, valid for 5 seconds.
  • Rookie: wider gaps. Hero: classic challenge. Legend: tight gaps and faster flight. Speed gradually increases.
  • Best scores are saved separately for each difficulty on this browser. Suits unlock at 15 and 40 lifetime sparks. No account needed.

Opening help or switching tabs pauses your run. Close this guide, then choose Resume. Gameplay is visual and requires timing.
